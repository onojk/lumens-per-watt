**What this is:** measurement only, from `explore/frontier.py` (bound rule committed before the run); data `results/frontier.json`; plot `results/frontier.png`.

**Measure:** exact lampcore charges; cost per factorisation C = total spent / successes, defined at ≥ half factored.

**How to read the cells:** each is the lamp's **worst-of ratio to the frontier**, max over op-class v1, ruint, Montgomery and the scaled gcd of C_lamp / (the per-pricing minimum over lamps).
- 1.00 means it holds the frontier under all four; `n/a` means not defined (f < 0.5, or not priceable under ruint and Montgomery: CFRAC); `–` means not run.
- In brackets: f (fraction factored) and log2 of the op-class C.
